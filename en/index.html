On this island,we do three things.

Starting from AI and connecting business, marketing and art: we read the books and material properly, try the methods by hand, and write up what happens in AI, one piece at a time.

  1. 01Read good books, explain them clearly.Every book on the shelf gets a card: why it is worth reading, where to read it legally, plus our mind maps, study cards and analysis.
  2. 02Try it by hand, write it down.Each prompt is actually run. The result sits next to the prompt, and whatever didn't work is written down too.
  3. 03Keep up, keep writing.The AI chronicle starts in 2017 and gains a new entry at every turning point. Old judgements stay; new findings are added after them.

Prompts

“Old Scroll · New Flower”, a 15-second film

Style: warm hand-drawn animation matching the scroll painting on the Onbloom Island home page, with watercolour and pencil texture. A seaside hillside in early morning, golden side light, slow and steady camera.

Characters:
· Xiangye: a boy of twelve or thirteen, dark curly hair, mustard-yellow jacket, beige scarf, carrying a pack.
· Atuo: a huge, gentle old tortoise with a low wooden scroll chest strapped to his back; a few long scrolls stick out of its opening.
· Lu: a round, chubby little creature made of orange-red clay, with a small opening in its belly and a glow of fire inside.
· Shi: a small white seabird with blue feathers.

Shots:
0–3 s: Atuo stops on the seaside hillside. Xiangye pulls an old long scroll from the chest and unrolls it; panel after small panel shows a wooden cradle holding a cup steady.
3–7 s: Lu holds a small cream-white clay cup with a crack and three mending staples, and places it in the wooden cradle, following the drawing. Shi lands on the cradle's crossbar and tilts its head.
7–11 s: A sea breeze blows and the cradle sways a little. Together they turn it to face the sun; the shoot in the cup slowly lifts and opens into a small flower.
11–15 s: In the blank panel at the end of the scroll, Xiangye draws the flower in charcoal (a drawing only, no words), rolls the scroll up and puts it back in the chest. The group walks on along the coastal path; the camera pulls back to show the sea and the distant hills.

Sound: waves, wind, a clay cup tapping wood, and a light xylophone note at the end. No dialogue, no narration.
Avoid: any text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks in frame; deformed characters, extra fingers.
Format: 16:9, 1080p.

        Why the prompt is written this way
        
          
          
            
        1. First say who it is and what it should do.One sentence on the role and the goal tells the AI where to put its effort.
          2. 
            
        2. Give it real material, then draw a line.You supply the experience and it is told not to invent any; where something is missing, it flags it and asks you.
          3. 
            
        3. Give it something to match against.With the job description, it knows which experience to put first.
          4. 
            
        4. Say exactly what you want back.Format, length, a comparison table, and finally a check of its own work.
          5.

        Privacy
        
        
        Onbloom Island currently has no accounts and no sign-in. We don't collect your name, email or any other personal information.
        
        
        Notes and saved items you keep under “Mine”, and answers you write under “Study together”, are stored only in this device's browser (local storage) and are never uploaded to any server. Clear your browser data or switch devices and they are gone.
        
        
        The site has no analytics, ads or third-party tracking code.
        
      
        
      
        
        
        Terms of use
        
        
        The content here is for learning and reference and is not professional advice. Each piece says what has and hasn't been tried; please judge by your own situation.
        
        
        The prompt templates are original first drafts written by us; you can copy them into the AI tool of your choice. The site itself runs no AI and makes no promises about the output.
        
        
        Please only put material you understand, and have the right to use, into the templates.
